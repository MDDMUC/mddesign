**Audience Focus:**
- From: Broad (anyone who needs design)

- **Rationale:** Speak directly to ideal clients, qualify leads, reduce tire-kickers

**3 Hero Projects:**
- Why 3: Each shows different strategic capability
- Why these: Strongest business outcomes, most strategic work, best proof points

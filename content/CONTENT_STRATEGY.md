## Case Study Template

Each case study should follow this structure to emphasize business outcomes and strategic thinking:

### Hero
- Project name
- Client logo
- One-line description
- Key metric or outcome
- Visual: Hero image or video

### Context (Problem)
- What was the business challenge?
- What was at stake?
- Why now?
- Quote from stakeholder (if available)

### Approach (Strategy)
- How did you think about the problem strategically?
- What was your process?
- Key insights from research
- Strategic decisions made

### Execution (Design)
- What did you actually create?
- Show the work with high-quality visuals
- Explain key design decisions
- Technical details (frameworks, tools, team size)

### Results (Outcomes)
- Measurable business outcomes
- Awards won
- What happened to the company/product after
- User feedback or testimonials
- Quote from client/stakeholder

### Visuals Throughout
- Lead with outcome/impact
- Show strategic artifacts (research, personas, frameworks) not just pixel-perfect mocks
- Before/after comparisons where relevant
- Process documentation
- Final product in context

## CEPRES Case Study Content Outline

**Hero:**
- "The world's leading private market investment platform"
- "$45 Trillion in assets • 6,000 LPs and GPs • 6 Private Equity Wire Awards"

**Context:**
- "CEPRES offered unparalleled access to granular private market data but lacked an efficient way to filter, analyze, and output data relevant to specific users."
- 76 different user personas, 125,000+ filtering options
- Product was becoming unusable due to complexity
- Risk of churn, competitive pressure

**Challenge:**
- "How do you create an intuitive dashboard experience when there are 125,000 ways to filter the data?"

**Approach:**
- "Consolidated 76 user personas into 7 broader use case scenarios"
- Extensive user research, interviews, synthesis of existing data
- Iterative design process: 7 complete platform iterations over 2+ years
- Collaborative workflow with market experts, fund managers, real users

**Design Decisions:**
- User-generated, custom branded dashboards
- Real-time analysis using live data
- Pre-defined and pre-selected filters based on use case
- Focus on reducing cognitive load while maintaining power

**Execution:**
- Full UI/UX redesign
- Custom dashboard builder
- Component library and design system
- Mobile and desktop responsive

**Results:**
- 6 Private Equity Wire Awards (Best Data Management 2019/2020, Best Portfolio Management 2018/2019/2020, Best Risk Management 2019)
- Adobe UI Design Excellence Showcase
- ILPA best practice due diligence partner
- Company grew from 15 to 120+ employees
- Offices expanded from Munich to New York, Singapore, Denver, London
- Today: $45T asset value, 6,000 LPs and GPs, 125,000 PE-backed companies, 12,000 funds managed

**Visuals:**
- Dashboard variations
- Before/after comparison
- User persona consolidation diagram
- Filter complexity diagram
- Final product screenshots
- Awards

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Overall Voice
- **Confident but not arrogant** - "I solve complex problems" not "I'm the best designer ever"
- **Direct and clear** - Executive communication, no fluff, respect their time
- **Business-focused** - Lead with outcomes, support with craft
- **Warm but professional** - Approachable expert, not cold consultant or overly casual

### Tone by Section

**Homepage:**
- Confident and compelling
- Clear value proposition within 5 seconds
- Professional but inviting

**Case Studies:**
- Strategic and analytical
- Show the thinking, not just the output
- Emphasis on outcomes and impact
- Technical where relevant but accessible

**About:**
- Personal but professional
- Show personality and values
- Build trust through credibility
- Hint at collaboration style

**Services:**
- Helpful and informative
- Clear about what you offer and how it works
- Remove friction from decision-making
- Confident in value, flexible in approach

### Words to Use
- Transform, strategic, outcomes, impact, growth, scale
- Partner, collaboration, embedded, leadership
- Award-winning, proven, measurable, results
- System, framework, process, foundation
- Ambitious, innovative, complex, sophisticated

### Words to Avoid
- Passionate (overused)
- Guru, ninja, rockstar (cliché)
- Disrupt (tired)
- Synergy, leverage (corporate speak)
- Beautiful, pretty, stunning (unless backed by awards/metrics)
